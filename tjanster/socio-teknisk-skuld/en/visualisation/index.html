How it all fits together

A single diagram of how socio-technical debt divides into social and technical debt, and how the register's 74 categories are distributed and arranged within each domain. Sub-categories are indented under their parent category. Every name links to the category's card in the register.

  • Social debt: debt in decisions, structures and relationships
  • Technical debt: debt in artefacts
  • Sub-category of the category above
  • Technical sub-category whose parent category is social
  • ★ Added to the register after the thesis

The diagram builds on figure 2 of the thesis, where the categories were sorted into two domains, and has been extended with the 29 categories added after the thesis and with the arrangement of 26 sub-categories. It is generated from the same data as the register and is updated whenever the register changes.
